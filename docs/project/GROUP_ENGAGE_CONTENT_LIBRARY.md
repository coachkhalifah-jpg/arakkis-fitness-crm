# Group Engage — Product-Owned Content Library

## Purpose and usage

This is the Product-owned evergreen library for Group Engage. These suggestions periodically give a coach a useful way to keep a Group active, connected, and informed. They are not automated messages, reminders, or tasks: the coach chooses whether to use one, edits it as needed, and sends it through the approved manual flow.

The notes are written to be pasted into a WhatsApp group or similar Group with little or no editing. They use coach-led, optional, plain language and avoid comparison, rankings, weight-loss pressure, calories, punishment, guilt, and public attendance shaming. Challenges do not require Arakkis tracking; people can participate by replying or practicing privately.

Field meanings:

- **Content name** — stable machine-friendly name for content identity; do not change casually after release.
- **Eyebrow** — short category label shown above the title.
- **Title** — concise coach-facing title.
- **Reason / purpose** — why this is useful now.
- **Suggested note** — editable coach-facing copy.
- **CTA** — approved action label for preparing the note.
- **Tags** — optional applicability hints for selection and future filtering.

## Duplicate and near-duplicate review

No exact duplicates were found within this initial library. The following near-neighbor pairs are intentionally distinct because they serve different coaching moments:

- `before-class-arrival-window` and `logistics-arrival-and-start-time`: the first sets a welcoming arrival window; the second confirms the complete timing schedule.
- `before-class-first-timer-welcome` and `before-class-community-introduction`: the first speaks directly to newcomers; the second helps a changing Group learn names.
- `before-class-equipment-check` and `logistics-what-venue-provides`: the first prompts personal preparation; the second clarifies Venue-provided equipment.
- `after-class-one-thing-noticed` and `after-class-small-win`: the first invites neutral observation; the second invites recognition of a positive change or effort.
- `after-class-recovery-check-in` and `after-class-recovery-menu`: the first opens a post-class conversation; the second offers a practical between-class experiment.
- `challenge-five-minute-mobility` and `tips-mobility-small-range`: the first is an optional shared practice; the second is evergreen education about how to approach mobility.
