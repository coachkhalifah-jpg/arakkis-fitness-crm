# Ownership and access checklists

## Ownership

- [ ] Git repository and organization are owned by the user/business.
- [ ] Vercel team and project are owned by the user/business.
- [ ] Supabase organization, staging project, and production project are owned by the user/business.
- [ ] Domain registrar and DNS account are owned by the user/business.
- [ ] Backup access and future monitoring provider are owned by the user/business.
- [ ] Future Stripe, messaging, or other service accounts are owned by the user/business if ever approved.
- [ ] No passwords or credentials are recorded in this checklist.

## Access and offboarding

- [ ] Invite developers with least privilege and staging-first access.
- [ ] Protect the production branch and require reviewed pull requests.
- [ ] Grant production access only for a documented need and time window.
- [ ] Review Git, Vercel, Supabase, and domain members regularly.
- [ ] Remove access at offboarding and rotate credentials/tokens that person could access.
- [ ] Confirm preview protection and synthetic-only data.
